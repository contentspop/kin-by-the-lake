01 · The idea

Golden hour to blue hour

Photography sells the experience more than any words. Every image should feel found, not staged, and warm, never cold.

Current images are renders from the design stage. Replace with KIN shoots when available.

Light

Shoot from 30 minutes before sunset to 30 after. Lanterns always lit. Available light plus one warm bounce, no flash. Keep the lake in every wide shot.

Food for sharing

Several plates in the middle, serving spoons in the dishes, plates part-eaten. House blue-and-white ceramics. One cuisine per frame.

People

Mid-conversation: hands reaching, a laugh caught side-on, mixed generations. No posing to camera, no clinking-glass clichés.

Grade

Warm highlights (3200 K feel), cool Lake Blue shadows, blacks lifted +4, saturation −8, greens toward olive, grain 2%. LUT: KIN_Lantern_v1.

Video

Locked-off or slow dolly only. 24 fps, 50% slow-mo for pours. Natural sound under one acoustic track. Cut on movement, hold each shot at least 2 seconds.

02 · Rules

Do and don’t

Do
  • Show the pavilion reflected in still water at dusk
  • Include a lit oil lantern on every table shot
  • Shoot hands sharing from the middle of the table
  • Use toile and tile as soft, out-of-focus context
  • Leave calm space (sky, water) for type
Don’t
  • Harsh midday sun, flash or cool-white LED
  • Single hero plates on empty white tables
  • Thai and Italian dishes “fused” in one styled shot
  • Teal-orange grading or HDR skies
  • Palm-frame postcard shots or a lantern-red flood
