Three Graphic Systems

KIN’s identity is a logo, three colours and two typefaces, with no pattern or illustration of its own yet. We propose three graphic languages that work alongside each other, not three competing looks: Ornamental Heritage, Illustrated Heritage and Expressive Ceramic Brushwork, joined by one line taken from the logo. The logo, the colours and the fonts stay exactly as they are. Part 2, menu materials and print finishes, follows.
