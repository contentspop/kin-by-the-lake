If creating visual artifacts (slides, mocks, throwaway prototypes, etc), copy assets out and create static HTML files for the user to view. If working on production code, you can copy assets and read the rules here to become an expert in designing with this brand.
If the user invokes this skill without any other guidance, ask them what they want to build or design, ask some questions, and act as an expert designer who outputs HTML artifacts _or_ production code, depending on the need.

Guiding mood (test every design against it): "A dining space where timeless patterns meet the warmth of nature. Indigo botanical fabrics, rich wood grains, and softly glowing lanterns come together beneath a canopy of greenery. Gentle curves and tactile materials create a sense of understated elegance, inviting guests to slow down, settle in, and enjoy the moment." Graphics stay calmer than the room; wood and greenery appear in photography only.

Key files: `styles.css` (tokens + fonts), `assets/logo/` (locked master logo + reflection strokes — never redraw), `assets/icons/` (line icons), `assets/patterns/` (Porcelain Lattice), `assets/photos/`, `components/`, `ui_kits/website/`, `templates/brand-book/BrandBook.dc.html` (full guidelines + quick reference).
