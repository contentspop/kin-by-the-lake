00 · Pick a direction

Three caption directions

Same photography, three ways of talking. All three keep the brand book guardrails: short sentences, no exclamation marks, no "fusion", Thai written for Thai readers rather than translated word for word. Each direction shows one KIN image and one Pinterest reference.

Direction A

The host

Warm, direct, short. Speaks like a host at the table. Closest to the brand book voice rules; safest for always-on posting.

Direction B

The storyteller

Heritage and craft, told slowly. Longer captions built on one true detail: porcelain, a recipe, a time of day. Builds depth and press appeal; posts less often.

Direction C

The table talk

Light and playful. Plays on the name (kin = eat, kin = family) and the small rules of sharing. Best for reach and Reels; needs a firm hand so it never tips into jokes about the food.
