01 · Principles

Two kitchens, one table, never fused

Drawn from the Brand Book (A6 Dining approach) and the positioning rules agreed on 22 October.

Phuket first

Phuket-Peranakan opens the menu and leads every headline. Italia follows.

Never fused

Each dish belongs to one kitchen. No dish appears in both, and there is no “fusion” section.

Made to share

Order a few, put them in the middle, pass them round, order a few more. Portions are sized for sharing.

One cuisine per plate

The two kitchens meet at the table, not on the plate. Pairings are shown side by side.

The menu is where the two-kitchen idea is most visible: a journey from Phuket to Italy, never a mix.

04 · Structure

How the menus are built

Main menu

A4 portrait, Lake Blue cloth cover, brass foil logo. Phuket & Peranakan pages first, then Italia, each opened by a reflection-stroke divider. Thai and English for every dish.

Drinks & wine

Separate book. Aperitivo → Cocktails (Thai-botanical, Italian classics) → Bollicine → Bianchi → Rossi → Dolci → Zero-proof.

Kids

A5 card, same type, no cartoons. Line icons to colour in on the back.

QR / digital

Brass table stand that opens the web menu for the current daypart. The printed menu is always offered.
