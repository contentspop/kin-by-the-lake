Peak to protect

Golden hour is the reason people book. Hold the best lake-edge tables for it and show it in every awareness campaign.

Hours to fill

15:00–17:00 is the quiet slot. An afternoon offer by the lake (coffee and dolci, kanom and tea) gives it a reason to exist.

One brand, many moods

Romantic for couples after dark, but the long family table stays at the heart of KIN. Content shows both.

Each moment is its own reason to visit. Campaigns, menus, photography and staffing all plan around these six.

The USP in one line

The only place in Phuket where a Phuket-Peranakan kitchen and an Italian kitchen cook for the same table, by the lake.

Draft. “Only” must be checked against the compset before it is used in public.

Each USP must pass four tests
  • True: it happens every day, not only at launch
  • Rare: no one in the compset (04) offers it
  • Wanted: it solves a real guest need
  • Provable: we can show it in a photo, a dish or a review

No single point is unbeatable on its own. The combination is the USP: both kitchens, done properly, at one table by the lake.

04 · Compset

Compared by location and ambience

Because KIN is setting-led, we compare it with the places guests choose for the same moment, not with other Thai or Italian restaurants.

Why not by cuisine

KIN is setting-led. Guests decide where to go by where it is and how it feels, then look at the menu. So our real competitors are the places people choose for the same moment, whatever they serve.

Two pillars

Location: where guests already are and the views they travel for.
Ambience: the mood of the moment, from a garden lunch to a candlelit dinner. Each one matches a moment in A day at KIN.
