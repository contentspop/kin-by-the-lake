00 · The core decision

Lead with the food, or with the lake?

From the activation workshop questions (22 October). Everything else on this page depends on this answer.

Cuisine-led

Hero is the Phuket dishes and the recipe and chef stories. Main compset is Phuket and Italian restaurants. The lake is the setting.

By the Lake

Hero is the lake and its moments: golden hour, after dark. Main compset is view and waterfront venues. The food is the reason to come back.

Hybrid · must be explicit

The lake brings first visits, the food builds repeat visits. Decide which channel and which guest leads with what, rather than saying everything everywhere.

This decides what every campaign shows first, which competitors matter most, and whether we sell a dish or a moment.

01 · Positioning lines

Earlier lines to reconcile

Option 1 · positioning

Real Phuket. Real Italian. By the lake.

ภูเก็ตแท้ อิตาเลียนแท้ ริมทะเลสาบ · Two kitchens, one standard: no mixing, no adapting.

Option 2 · tagline / visuals

One lake. Two kitchens.

ทะเลสาบเดียว สองครัวให้เลือก

Option 3 · campaigns / ads

Everyone’s favourite, at one lakeside table.

ทุกคนได้เมนูที่ชอบ ในโต๊ะเดียวริมทะเลสาบ

Rules
  • Phuket comes first in every headline
  • No fusion, in the menu or in content
  • Keep a clear visual cue for each kitchen

The Brand Book essence (“From the long lunch to the last lantern…”) is the idea. These lines are candidates for campaigns and ads, to choose once the core decision is made.

03 · Audience

Who comes, for what

Hypothesis

Phuket families and friends, curious visitors, and occasions and groups. See Brand Book · A5.

Key question

Also check
  • Which group comes for the food, which for the lake
  • Which spends most, which brings the biggest tables
  • What stops new guests: awareness, “Thai or Italian?” confusion, price

05 · Activation

Bring new guests without discounting

Idea

A ritual by the lake

A daily time-of-day moment, such as lighting the lanterns at sunset, that people plan a visit around.

Idea

Recipe heritage

Stories of Phuket recipes and their families, and the Italian chef’s origins, as an ongoing content series.

Idea

Two kitchens, one table

A monthly sharing menu that puts both kitchens on the table.

Idea

Try the other kitchen

A LINE OA follow-up that invites first-time guests back for the kitchen they did not order from.

06 · Method

Restaurant observation framework

Pick 2–3 direct competitors in each arena plus 3–5 restaurants to observe, and compare them on the same criteria. Full workshop questions are on the KIN page in Notion.

CriteriaWhat to evaluate
PositioningConcept, USP, brand identity
Target audienceLocal, expat, tourist, hotel guests
PricingAverage spend, menu pricing, value
Menu & productSignature dishes, menu innovation
MarketingCampaigns, promotions, collaborations
Digital presenceInstagram, TikTok, Google reviews
Guest experienceAtmosphere, service, presentation
Setting & time of dayHow they use the view to sell a moment
Authenticity storyHow they prove “the real thing”
Concept clarityHow multi-concept venues separate each concept
ActivationEvents, themed nights, offers
Competitive advantageWhat they do particularly well
OpportunityWhat we can learn or do differently

Don’t stop at “who are our competitors?” Ask what they do well, why it works, and what that opens up for KIN.
